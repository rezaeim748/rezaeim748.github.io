Hi, I’m **Mohammadreza Rezaei**, a graduate student in Computer Science at **TU Dresden**.

My main interests include **machine learning**, **data science**, **graph neural networks**, and intelligent systems. I enjoy developing practical implementations of machine-learning concepts and exploring how data-driven methods can solve real-world problems.

I received my bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering from **Amirkabir University of Technology** and am currently pursuing my master’s degree at TU Dresden.
